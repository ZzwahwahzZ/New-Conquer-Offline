## Accepted license policy

- **CC0 1.0** is accepted for commercial use, modification and redistribution.
- **CC BY 4.0** is accepted when the creator, exact asset title, source URL and license URL are retained, and changes are disclosed.
- CC BY-SA, NC, ND, custom restricted licenses and unclear license records are not automatically accepted.
- Every included file must retain source, creator, license, byte count and SHA-256 in its manifest.
